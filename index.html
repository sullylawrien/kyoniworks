// About & Services / Sobre

The Studio

KyoniWorks is a Live2D rigger dedicated to bringing VTuber models to life with fluid movement, expressive physics, and polished toggles.

Every rig is built with care — from jiggle physics and fluffy ear sways to complex expression toggles that make your model feel truly alive on stream.
